Use the W, A, S, D keys to navigate menus and move your heart during gameplay.

Use Space or Enter to confirm selections, toggle settings, or perform actions inside specific hells.
ESC
Press Escape to pause the game or go back to the previous menu.

The Time Gauge

Your health is literally your Time. Taking damage subtracts seconds from your clock. If your clock hits zero, it's game over! Survive as long as you can across the shifting hells.

Practice Modes

Want to master a specific hell? Head over to the MODE option on the Start Page to practice any unlocked hell in isolation!
